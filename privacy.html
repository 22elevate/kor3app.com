Privacy Policy

KOR3 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

22 Elevate, LLC ("we", "us") operates the KOR3 app and kor3app.com. This policy explains what information is handled when you use them. In short: KOR3 does not require an account, and your tasks stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your name (if you enter one), your daily tasks, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive or store this information on our servers.

Purchase information

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to verify your subscription status. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier, and may receive device and app information needed to process purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's, and Google's.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, KOR3 schedules reminders locally on your device. You can turn them off anytime in your device settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, run advertising, or use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in the app. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and KOR3 is intended for adults 18 and older.

Website and email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to respond. The website may collect standard server logs (such as IP address) through its hosting provider.

Security

Data in transit to RevenueCat is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you delete the app. To request deletion of purchase-related data held by us or our processors, email hello@22elevate.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including California and the EEA/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the address below to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
